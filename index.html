Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Glasify ("we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our App, including facial scan images used to generate your skin analysis.

2. Information We Collect

  • Face Scan Images: photos you capture or upload for analysis. These may constitute biometric or health-adjacent data in some jurisdictions.
  • Scan Results: SkinScore, SkinAgeScan, Glow Profile, and routine data generated from your images. Your most recent scan result is stored on our servers so it can be restored if you reinstall the App and sign in again.
  • Account Data: your Apple ID identity (via Sign in with Apple), used solely to create a persistent account tied to you rather than to a single device, and subscription status (processed via Apple's StoreKit; we do not receive your full payment card details).
  • Optional Profile Data: date of birth (used only to compute your age for result personalization and an optional birthday notification), if you choose to provide it, to improve result accuracy.
  • Referral Payout Data: if you choose to set up referral payouts, you enter your email address and country in the App. Your identity and bank details are entered directly with our payout provider Stripe — never with us. We store only a reference to your Stripe account and the commission you have earned and been paid.

3. How We Use Face Scan Images

Your face scan image is transmitted securely to our backend, which forwards it to our AI analysis provider (Anthropic's Claude API) solely to generate your skin analysis results. We do not use your face images to train third-party AI models, and we do not sell your face images.

4. Storage & Retention

Face scan images themselves are not retained after your results are generated. Your scan results (SkinScore, SkinAgeScan, Glow Profile, and routine data — not the underlying photo) are stored on our servers so your most recent result can be restored to your account, including after a reinstall, until you delete them. You may request deletion of your scan history at any time (see Section 9).

5. Biometric-Adjacent Data & Consent

Facial scan images, and any pre-extracted skin metrics derived from them, may constitute biometric or health-adjacent data under some laws (for example, the EU GDPR's special category data provisions, or Illinois' Biometric Information Privacy Act). We process this data only on the basis of your explicit, informed consent, captured via a timestamped confirmation you actively check before your first scan — this consent record is stored server-side as evidence.

You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account and scan history in Settings, which also deletes your stored scan results and derived metrics from our systems within a reasonable period.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We share data with:

  • Our AI analysis provider (Anthropic), to process scan images and generate results.
  • Apple, via Sign in with Apple and StoreKit, to authenticate you and process subscription purchases (we do not receive your payment credentials).
  • Our database provider (Supabase), to securely store your account and scan status.
  • RevenueCat, which manages subscription status on our behalf, receiving your account identifier and purchase events from Apple (not your payment credentials).
  • Affiliate retail partners, including YesStyle, ONLY in aggregate or click-level form necessary to track affiliate referrals (e.g., that a referral occurred) — we do not share your face images or full scan results with retail affiliate partners.
  • Stripe, our payout provider, ONLY if you set up referral payouts — Stripe collects your identity and bank details directly, under its own privacy policy (stripe.com/privacy), to verify you and send your commission.
  • If you participate in our referral program, users you refer see only your referral code; you see only aggregate status (active/trial/churned) about users you referred, never their personal data or scan results.

We do not sell your personal data to data brokers.

7. Legal Basis & International Transfers

Where GDPR or similar law applies, we process face scan images based on your explicit consent (given at the time of scanning), given the sensitive nature of biometric-adjacent data. Data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, using appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Notifications

If you enable notifications, we schedule local, on-device reminders (ReplenishmentLoop) based on your estimated product usage. This scheduling happens on your device; we do not need to access your notification interactions to provide reminders.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction or deletion of your data (including scan results and payout details); object to or restrict certain processing; and request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@glasify.app. You can also delete your scan history directly within the App under Settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Glasify is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect face scan images or other personal data from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us so we can delete it.

11. Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated in-app or via the update notes on the App Store.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions or data requests, contact privacy@glasify.app.

14. Cosmetic & Educational Disclaimer

Reminder: all results generated from your face scan images (SkinAgeScan, SkinScore, Glow Profile, and routine recommendations) are cosmetic and educational estimates only. They are not a medical diagnosis and this policy does not change that. See our Terms of Use for the full medical disclaimer.
